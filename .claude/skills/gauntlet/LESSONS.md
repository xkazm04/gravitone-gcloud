# Lessons — gauntlet

## 1.0 — 2026-08-12 — gravitone-gcloud (run 1, L1 first sweep, 20 seats, 187 findings)

Applied in v1.1 unless marked otherwise.

- **The content/lens axis needed a third value, and its absence corrupted the headline number.**
  v1.0 offered `content | lens | undecided`. 105 of 187 findings were "the shared mechanism is wrong
  for everyone" — which is neither. They were filed `content` because it was the only remaining
  label, and `content` reads as *"the methodic is fine, it was fed the wrong material"*. The run's
  own tally therefore understated its finding until the judge re-read it. Added `mechanism`.
  *(Applied.)*

- **Never score occupancy when you mean sorting.** The column-utilisation dial reported `7/7 used`
  for a seat whose board had absorbed 77% of the topic into one column. 17 of 20 seats filled 6–7 of
  7, so the dial looked reassuring for the entire run while the real defect count piled onto the
  schema. Replaced with orphan count + max-column concentration. Generalises to any skill measuring
  a taxonomy's fit: *a container that is full has not necessarily sorted anything.* *(Applied.)*

- **The judge must be empowered to reject the question, not only the answers.** The run was built to
  ask "which domains need their own lens?" and returned zero lenses across four areas, with the two
  axes that actually predict fit (evidence age, subject exposure) turning out to be per-run
  declarations rather than packs. A judge briefed only to grant-or-deny lenses would have granted
  some. Instruct it that ruling the *concept* wrong is an available verdict. *(Applied.)*

- **The HYPOTHESIS-labelling discipline inherited from `/uat` paid for itself on its first outing,
  and should never be softened.** Three of the orchestrator's four leads were wrong; the first
  returning walker refuted one with citations rather than confirming it, and the mid-run retraction
  redirected the remaining seats at a *larger* finding than the lead had pointed to. Two seats
  disclosed incidental grep exposure to the forbidden hypothesis file, unprompted, and both reached
  conclusions contradicting the leaked lines — which the judge then weighted accordingly. The cost
  of the discipline is a paragraph; the value is that a wrong lead cannot become the run's finding.

- **Seat construction: build seats that can refute their own reason for existing.** The four
  "structurally hostile" seats produced the run's sharpest results *and* two of its cleanest
  refutations — the seat built to prove the evidence ladder had no rung for interpretive work found
  the rung, hunted for the downstream rule that would demote it, and reported finding none. A
  hostile seat that can only confirm its own hardship is a rigged seat.

### Redesign proposal — NOT applied in v1.1

**L1's charitable-reading defence is weaker than the method claims, and one walker proved it.** The
skill says L1 "reads the prompt charitably" and that L2 exists to catch the gap. Run 1 found a
mandatory requirement that had **failed open on the reference artifact itself** — searched-never,
adjudicated-anyway, self-certified twice — which L1 caught by reading the *shipped output* rather
than the instruction. Proposal for a future version: make **"walk the worked reference and check
whether each mandate actually held"** an explicit numbered L1 step, not an optional reading. It
converts L1 from "would this instruction work?" to "did it?", at no browser cost. Not applied
because it changes the level's definition and deserves its own run to validate.
