# Scout-muse lessons

- 2026-10-23-3 · A retro game-render look (PS1 low-poly) plus a long single braid and an action jacket drew a franchise heroine's silhouette; the eye checks real people only. When a look names a game era, give the card a hair and garment far from that era's iconic heroines. Argues a line in `knowledge/scout-muse/techniques.md` (## The line).
- 2026-10-23-3 · `Adult proportions, a poised mature expression` did not hold the age on print/anime/low-poly looks (a 44-year-old card read 25). Age marks are out (1.2.0: no grey hair, wrinkles or laugh lines), so carry adulthood by styling instead: a grown-up silhouette, adult wardrobe and profession props, an adult hairstyle, a composed gaze. Argues a change in techniques.md (## The look ladder, rung 5).
