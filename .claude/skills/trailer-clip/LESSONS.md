# trailer-clip lessons

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-05 - gravitone-gcloud
- H3 clips carry real sound (Unlatched h3-v2: AAC 32 kHz stereo, mean -42.2 to -14.0 dB by clip), and `assemble.mts` discards it, so the trailer has its own cutter. Tested on copies of two clips: 197 frames as planned, a 2.5 s black exact at 2.5-5.0 s, -91 dB across it.
- Native audio first, operator's order: a custom layer (probably ElevenLabs) only after the native cut proves the quality. A `--cover` mix was built and removed the same day for that reason.
- RTAI, first art round (Codex, 55-75 s an image): "ghost workers" drew humans, and a robot described by its paint and visor drew a man in armor with cloth trousers. Round 2 said it outright ("no human face, no skin, no clothes"; "unmistakably a machine, no fabric, bare pistons and cable bundles") and both held across all seven images. Word a non-human cast by what it must not have, and fix the cast sheet first: every image attaches it.
- RTAI keyframes (2026-11-06, 56 Codex images, about 50-100 s each): one plate plus a sheet held one lit place across six concepts, and end frames painted from their start with "keep the camera, lens, distance and framing exactly" kept the framing. Review caught what validation cannot: "workers" drew humans again wherever the sentence did not say robots; a security camera saw itself; a low angle turned a shoulder into a second head; and a shot framed near the tanks had its blast erupt elsewhere. Say where the event happens relative to the camera, and "robots" in every sentence naming a crowd.
- An idle card does not read as idle in memory: ComfyUI keeps its last models loaded (34 GB at 3%). The 12 GB gate only reads true after /free, so day-run.ps1 frees first and then reads it.
