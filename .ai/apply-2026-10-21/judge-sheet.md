# Blind judging sheet: four idea sets for one brief

Brief: Rainshell, a packable rain jacket. Audience: city cyclists who commute all year.
Proposition: "This jacket keeps you dry in a downpour." Tone: deadpan. 15-second vertical social ad.

Four sets (W, X, Y, Z), six ideas each. Each idea: title, then its creative leap.

## Set W
W1. A waiter crosses a drenched wedding lawn and lifts the cloche: a dry shirt, served. Leap: the cloche stands in for the jacket; under the dome is a commuter's dry shirt; cut to a cyclist unzipping with the same flourish.
W2. A 100% rain forecast, and the fourteen-year-old grins. Leap: the forecast that cancels everyone's plans is good news for him; the downpour empties the bike lane; he arrives dry while the bus queue is soaked.
W3. The courier takes the shortcut through the automatic car wash. Leap: he rides straight through soap, brushes and rinse jets and hands over a bone-dry envelope, face unmoved.
W4. The lost-property tissue test. Leap: a teacher pins a tissue inside a cotton hoodie, a fleece and the jacket in a lost-property room and pours a watering can; tissues come out soggy, soggy, dry; the jacket is tagged "claimed".
W5. The housekeeper rides through dawn rain and starts her shift without a trace of it. Leap: no joke, no test; quiet pride; she unzips to a crisp uniform, packs the jacket away, smooths her apron like a bed.
W6. A new father times the gaps between rain showers with a kitchen timer. Leap: he sprints awning to awning on a four-minute timer, gets soaked anyway, resets; a cyclist in the jacket rides past dry.
Longlist: The commute served under a silver cloche / Dry shirt plated like the main course / Rain tent over the head table / The rain forecast is the good news / Teen cheers at a wall of rain / Forecast map rains on everyone but him / Courier takes the car-wash shortcut / Delivered dry through a waterfall / Riding under a burst water main / Lost-property tissue test under the leak / Watering-can lesson at the lost-property shelf / Every soaked jacket except one in lost-property / Housekeeper rides through dawn rain, starts dry / Nobody can tell she rode here / Crisp like the bed she just made / Father times rain gaps with kitchen timer / Four-minute sprints between awnings / Baby schedule logic applied to the sky

## Set X
X1. The office towel stack nobody touches. Leap: a housekeeper restocks the cyclists' towel shelf every rainy morning; every evening it is untouched; the stack reaches the ceiling; the cyclist passes with a small packed bundle.
X2. No room on the drying rack, and no need. Leap: a new father's rack is crammed with baby clothes; he comes in from a storm commute with nothing to dry and hangs one small packed bundle on a hook.
X3. Everyone's on hold except her. Leap: after a late shift, call-centre workers in headsets wait out the rain in the lobby like a hold queue; one pulls a bundle from her pocket and rides off.
X4. The awning queue is not for fish. Leap: the queue under a fishmonger's awning is people sheltering from rain; only the cyclist at the back buys, pulls a jacket from her pocket and rides off.
X5. She takes the car wash home. Leap: a night-shift petrol-station student watches, unimpressed, as a regular rides her bike through the automatic car wash, packs the jacket into its pocket and leaves.
X6. The staffroom photo: drenched then, dry now. Leap: a faded staffroom photo of a young teacher soaked on a bike; a new photo pinned beside it, same bike, same rain, a packed bundle in her hand, children unimpressed.
Longlist: The towels stay folded all winter / Turndown service for a jacket / The office towel stack keeps growing / No room on the rack, no need / Everything in the baby bag, plus one / Only the baby clothes are drying / Everyone's on hold except her / The lobby waits for the rain / Shift ends, rain doesn't, she leaves / The awning queue is not for fish / Market shelter queue, one cyclist buys / Fish on ice, cyclist in rain / She takes the car wash home / The canopy loses its regular / Same cyclist, every wet night, gone / The class photo, soaked, and now / Staffroom wall: then drenched, now dry / Rainy school trip, yellow coats, years later

## Set Y
Y1. Your commute, delivered like pressed hotel laundry. Leap: the rider is treated like hotel laundry sealed in thin film and carried through the storm; the film peeled at the office is the jacket; the shirt under it is dry.
Y2. He cycled in through a storm; the only wet patch is the baby's. Leap: a father at his desk, dry, rain on the window, one damp patch of baby milk on his shoulder; the jacket drips on his chair.
Y3. After the late shift, the rain is so heavy fish swim the bike lane. Leap: the street is effectively underwater, fish and a floating cone drift past; she pedals through unbothered; at home she unzips, dry.
Y4. The paper-wrapped parcel that rode home dry under the jacket. Leap: a fishmonger hands over fish in brown paper; the cyclist tucks it under the jacket, rides home through the downpour; the paper unfolds crisp.
Y5. At the end of the night shift, she steps out from under the canopy without waiting. Leap: everyone shelters under a petrol-station canopy; she steps into the rain without breaking stride, rides home at dawn.
Y6. Class photo, years ago: the teacher at the back in a full wetsuit and snorkel. Leap: she once cycled to school dressed for diving every rainy year; today's photo shows her in the jacket, ordinary, dry.
Longlist: Ride in like pressed hotel laundry / Turn-down service for your commute / Hospital corners under a downpour / The only wet patch is the baby's / Dry father, damp burp cloth / Home through rain so deep fish follow / On hold with the sky all night / Raining so hard the bus lane swims / The paper parcel that rode home dry / Last in the fish queue, still dry / Out from the canopy without waiting / The shift ends; the rain doesn't matter / Nobody waits out the rain at dawn / Class photo, 2009: teacher in a wetsuit / Every school year, a different shower curtain

## Set Z
Z1. Served dry: a waiter lifts a silver cloche off a cyclist in the rain. Leap: a huge catering cloche on a flooded street; lifted, a bone-dry cyclist underneath; the jacket is then stuffed into a pocket-sized pouch.
Z2. The forecast's storm cloud follows one rider and gets nowhere. Leap: a flat forecast-symbol cloud follows one cyclist and pours on them all the way; they get off dry and unbothered; the cloud hovers, out of ideas.
Z3. The courier unzips in a cloudburst and hands over a dry parcel. Leap: the parcel was never in the bag but inside the jacket; the courier pulls it out dry and rides off into the downpour.
Z4. Lost property: a wall of forgotten umbrellas, and not one Rainshell. Leap: umbrellas get left behind; this jacket packs into its pocket and never leaves the rider, so its shelf is empty; cut to a dry cyclist outside.
Z5. A housekeeping cart of fresh towels waits at the bike rack, unused. Leap: the office lays on hotel-scale towel service for soaked cyclists; one rider arrives dry and walks past; the housekeeper refolds the towel.
Z6. A new dad races a kitchen timer home through a downpour, dry. Leap: the timer by a warming bottle is his deadline; he walks in dry before it rings, picks up the bottle; the drama is that there is no drama.
Longlist: The cloche lifted on a soaked street / Served dry: the rider under the dome / Rain on the marquee, dry at the door / Ignore the forecast, it's in your pocket / The forecast cloud rains on one rider / Weather map, rider rides straight through it / The courier is the waterproof parcel / Delivered dry, from inside the jacket / Ten hours of rain, one dry shoulder / A thousand umbrellas, no jackets lost / Lost property: everything but the Rainshell / The shelf of drowned, forgotten umbrellas / The towel left folded on the chair / A housekeeping cart waiting at the bike rack / Fresh towels for everyone, one rider declines / Home before the timer rings, still dry / Four minutes, one downpour, one dry dad / The bottle timer and the cloudburst
