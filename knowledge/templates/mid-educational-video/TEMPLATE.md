# Template — mid-length educational video (3–6 min)

Subjects: **economy · tech · politics.** The format where an idea gets enough room to be argued
rather than asserted, and short enough that every beat still has to earn its place.

## Why this length is the studio's centre of gravity

At 1–3 minutes an idea gets **one** turn and no room to be wrong in an interesting way. At 15+ it
needs a sponsor, a disclaimer beat and several deep-dives to sustain itself. **3–6 minutes is the
shortest length that holds a full argument** — a claim, a mechanism, a real objection, and a verdict —
which is exactly what economy/tech/politics subjects require to be honest.

## What makes this template hard

1. **Enough room to ramble.** The 1–3 minute format enforces discipline by brutality. At 5 minutes a
   weak script survives long enough to bore someone. The turn budget is the guard.
2. **The subjects are contested.** Economy, tech and politics all have a "side", which makes
   Adjudication the natural engine *and* the easiest one to fake — see `ENGINES.md` § D-honest vs
   D-rigged. **This is the template where the honesty rules earn their keep.**
3. **Currency decays.** Tech and politics scripts are dated by construction (Fireship says the date
   out loud). The library should treat "how long is this true for" as a property of the script.
