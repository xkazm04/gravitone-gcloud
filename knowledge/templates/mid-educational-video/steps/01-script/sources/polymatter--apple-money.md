## What transfers

1. **The promise as three topics** (0:23), executed in order. Half the words of a question stack.
2. **Surfacing the hidden premise** (5:40) — attacking the unstated assumption rather than the
   conclusion. The most respectful and most devastating move in the corpus, and it needs mid-length.
3. **A rate as the opening number.** "$1,444 per second" is felt; "$45bn a year" is not.
4. **Institutions given dialogue** — Ireland rolls its eyes, Apple winks at the IRS. Turns tax law
   into characters with motives.
5. **The self-attack at 4:10** on a subject where the audience's sympathy is against the subject.
6. **The reframe close** re-describes the whole video as a virtue: saying no *is* the strategy.
