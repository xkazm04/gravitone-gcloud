# Open questions — short-form clip script step

---

### s1 · What survives compression from 40s to ≤30s? ⭐ blocking the whole template
**Nothing in this template is measured below 40 seconds**, and the brief asks for 30. The Anchor
Ladder in particular is unlikely to keep five rungs; three is the plausible short form and is a guess.
*Settles it:* 3–4 clips in the 20–30s band, from any channel doing it well. **This is the first thing
the next research run should fetch** — everything else here is refinement.

### s2 · Is the derived-short contract right? ⭐
Four rules, one witness. In particular rule 2 — *the withheld thing must be a different question* — is
the least obvious and most load-bearing, and it comes from a single example.
*Settles it:* 3–4 more clips that are demonstrably cut from a known long-form parent. Channels that
post both are easy to find; the useful comparison is the clip against its parent's transcript.

### s3 · Does the closing joke generalise, or is it Fireship's habit?
Observed in 2 of 3, both from the same channel. PolyMatter closes on a pointer instead.
*Settles it:* shorts from three more channels. Also worth asking whether a *non-comic* short can close
without a reframe at all, or whether it needs some other memorability device.

### s4 · Does "did you know" actually underperform, or is it just absent from good work?
The claim in `PATTERNS.md` §1 is that the announced-fact opening is weak. The evidence is only that
none of the three studied shorts uses it — an absence, not a measurement.
*Settles it:* honestly, engagement data the studio does not have. Until then the rule stands as a
reasoned preference, not a finding, and should be labelled that way when it reaches the UI.

### s5 · Can a short run an Adjudication or a Reversal Chain?
Only three engines observed at this length, and all three are structurally simple. Whether a genuine
turn — settle, signal, mechanism, escalate — fits in 30 seconds is unknown, and MinutePhysics'
18-second reversals suggest it might.
*Settles it:* a short built on a contested claim rather than a fact or a mechanism.

### s6 · How is the parent chosen?
The workflow is "shorts scoped from mid-length content", but nothing here says *which* 30 seconds of a
5-minute script should become a clip. Candidate heuristics: the most concrete detail; the beat with
the strongest standalone hook; the turn the parent spends least time on.
*Settles it:* comparing several derived clips against their parents to see which beat was chosen.
Depends on s2's corpus.

## Not asked

Engagement optimisation — hook A/B patterns, retention curves, thumbnail/title interaction. The studio
has no measurement channel for any of it, and encoding vendor blog claims as craft rules would put
unverifiable numbers into a library whose whole value is that its claims are traceable.
