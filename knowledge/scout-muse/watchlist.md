# Muse watchlist

What the muse wing looks for on the web (spark scout-muse): craft, never a person. A source seeds
words; it is never passed to the studio.

- tag: portrait lighting patterns - Rembrandt, butterfly, loop, split, clamshell, on women of every skin tone
- tag: skin micro-texture in generated portraits - what breaks the procedural glow
- tag: fashion editorial framing and fabric behaviour - drape, sheen, silhouette
- tag: anime character sheets with adult proportions - line weight, cel shadow, mature expression
- tag: stylised 3D character rendering - subsurface skin, hair shading, toon outlines
- tag: real-time avatar concept art - turnarounds, expression sheets, dialogue portraits
- tag: text-only character consistency - identity blocks, one change at a time
- tag: poster typography space - negative space and eye lines in 4:5 key art
