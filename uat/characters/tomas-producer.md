# Tomás — the agency producer

**Segment:** non-technical. Runs delivery at a 6-person content studio, ships
4–6 client explainers a month against fixed slot lengths.

**Background / voice.** Not a writer. His job is that the thing lands on the
date, at the length the client bought, and does not get the client sued. He has
one hard constraint everyone else treats as soft: **a 5:00 slot is 5:00.** Talks
in deadlines and deliverables. Reads a script once, for risk.

**Job to be done.** Take a writer's draft, apply the client's notes ("more on
the ETF part, drop the company stuff"), and get a version he can send back —
without reading the whole argument himself.

**What good looks like.** He pastes the client's notes in as instructions, runs
one pass, and gets something that either fits the slot or tells him plainly that
it does not and by how much.

**Pet peeves.** A tool that silently rescales to make numbers fit. Anything that
requires him to understand the argument to use it. Losing a client note because
the UI ate it.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: relaying client notes to a writer and waiting for a revision is
**a day and a half of calendar time**, and two rounds is normal. The tool must
turn a batch of notes into a reviewable revision in **one sitting, under 15
minutes**, or he keeps using the writer.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
The revision must be at least as safe as one a senior producer would sign off:
it fits the slot or says by how much it does not, it did not drop a legal or
factual safeguard to make room, and every client note is visibly accounted for —
applied, or explicitly refused with a reason.

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. He can enter several notes and run ONE pass over all of them.
2. Every note he entered is visibly accounted for in the result.
3. A result that no longer fits the slot says so in seconds, not percentages.
4. Nothing is silently rescaled to make it fit.
5. He cannot accidentally start two revisions of the same project.

**Surface binding.** Step 2 Script (Coverage, Spend bar, the pad). Opens the
notebook only when something looks wrong.
