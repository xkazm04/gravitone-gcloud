# Priyanka — the course-module creator

**Segment:** in-house L&D, non-technical, team of two. Produces 2–3 minute
training modules for a 4,000-person company; ships 8 a month against a
compliance calendar.

**Background / voice.** Instructional designer. Every module has a learning
objective, a check, and a legal reviewer. Uses Articulate, Vyond and a shared
drive. Does not care about "argument" — cares about one idea, explained once,
correctly, on time. Reads every generated sentence for liability. Warm, tidy,
process-minded.

**Job to be done.** Create a short educational module, scope the material so
only vetted facts travel, and hand a candidate script to legal with a record of
what was kept and cut.

**What good looks like.** The scope decisions are a record she can point to.
The steel-man / required material rule is explained, not enforced silently.
The Coverage view answers "which facts does this script use".

**Pet peeves.** Content she cannot attribute. A checkpoint that "confirms"
something and then quietly drifts. Being sent to a library to "commission a
style" when she just wants a script.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: research, outline, draft and a legal pre-read for a 2-minute
module is **~5 hours**. She will adopt a tool that gets her a scoped, sourced
candidate in **under 45 minutes**, with the scope record legal can read.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
A senior instructional designer refuses a script with a claim the evidence log
does not carry, and refuses any workflow where the approved scope and the
script disagree without a warning. The confirm/drift mechanism must say when
the board has moved since confirmation.

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. The scope checkpoint states what will travel and reports drift after.
2. Every card she cuts stays cut in Script's Coverage view.
3. Required material is explained on the card that cannot be cut.
4. Conclusions default OUT and say so — nothing speculative travels by
   default.
5. The evidence log is reachable from both Research and Script.

**Surface binding.** `/projects/new` (educational → short-educational-video →
preset), Research guided (takes, conclusions, review) and expert board, Script
Coverage + Candidates. Never Frames.

**References.** L&D module production practice (Articulate/Vyond, compliance
cadence). Training-data grounded, marked.
