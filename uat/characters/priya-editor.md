# Priya — the standards editor

**Segment:** semi-technical. Fact-checks and clears scripts before publication
for a mid-size media brand. Sign-off is hers.

**Background / voice.** Spent four years at a newsroom where a single unsourced
number triggered a correction and a very long meeting. She reads for the join
between a claim and its support, and she is the person who says no. Blunt.
Assumes any generated text is wrong until shown otherwise.

**Job to be done.** Confirm that a script — including one a machine just
rebalanced — says nothing the research does not support, and that the limits the
research declared are still being honoured.

**What good looks like.** Every claim in the script traces to a fact she can
open. The notebook's declared constraints ("never state a precise price",
"say moves with, not because of") are checked against the script she is actually
clearing, not against an earlier draft.

**Pet peeves.** A green tick that was computed against a different version.
"Checks passed" with no statement of what was checked. Being handed a regenerated
draft alongside the *original's* verification.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: clearing a 5-minute script — pulling every figure, checking every
causal verb, confirming the counter-case is present — is **2–3 hours**. The tool
must cut that to **under 45 minutes** by doing the mechanical passes for her.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
The verification shown against a script must be *of that script*. A check that
was computed against a previous version and displayed against the current one is
worse than no check, because it manufactures confidence she would not otherwise
have. **She would rather see "unverified" than a tick she cannot trust.**

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. Every check on screen states which version it was computed against.
2. A rebalanced script is not shown with the baseline's verification.
3. The declared constraints are re-checked, or shown as unchecked.
4. A load-bearing claim at low confidence is visible before sign-off.
5. Nothing claims to be verified that was not.

**Surface binding.** Step 1 (evidence log, notebook), Step 2 (Candidates,
Coverage). Does not use the Spend bar.
