# Sofia — the short-film director

**Segment:** small team, craft-first, external prospect. Directing a 15-minute
festival short; needs a ≤60 s teaser for the crowdfunding page before the
footage exists.

**Background / voice.** Film-school, two shorts in festivals, an editor
friend who cuts for her at night. Thinks in images and withholding: "what do
we NOT show". Distrusts anything that spells out plot. Has been burned by an AI
storyboard tool that flattened her tone into "cinematic". Speaks in references
(Denis, Reichardt) and refuses templates that flatten them.

**Job to be done.** Compose a teaser spine that holds back — one hook, tone
and imagery, no plot — and see the withholding budget as a thing she controls,
before anyone renders a frame.

**What good looks like.** The teaser template is honest that it is ≤60 s, one
hook, light on story. The withholding panel lets her set allowances. The
promise ledger shows what a beat promises and who pays it.

**Pet peeves.** A cut that spells out the ending. "Cinematic" used as a
synonym for "nice". A style stage that offers only explainer looks for a film
teaser and calls that a choice.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: a teaser beat sheet argued out with her editor over two
evenings — **~3 hours** of two people. She will adopt a tool that gets her a
composed, withholding-aware teaser spine in **under 30 minutes**, provided it
does not decide her tone for her.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
A senior trailer editor refuses a teaser with more than one hook, refuses one
that pays its own promise, and refuses a "teaser" that is a trailer cut short.
The teaser spine must be visibly a different contract from the trailer's, not
the trailer's rungs trimmed.

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. A teaser project can be created on a fresh account with a style that is
   honest about what it was written for.
2. The teaser's research/spine is visibly different from a full trailer's —
   fewer parts or a stated shortening rule — or the board says it is not.
3. Every promise in the composed cut names a payer, or says none is named.
4. The withholding budget is editable and its allowances are explained.
5. Nothing on the path asks her to render or pick an image.

**Surface binding.** `/projects/new` (trailer → teaser → style stage), Research
BeatVariantBoard, Script TrailerScript (WithholdingPanel, PromiseLedger). Never
Frames.

**References.** Teaser vs trailer vs cinematic as different contracts
(repo's own craft library, `knowledge/templates/teaser`); festival teaser
practice. Training-data grounded, marked.
