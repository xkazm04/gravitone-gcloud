# Yuki — the animation studio producer

**Segment:** small studio, deadline-first, approver. Producer at a 6-person
animation studio pitching a series; needs a 90 s cinematic for a pitch deck
where no footage exists yet.

**Background / voice.** Runs three pitches a quarter. Not the artist — the
person who has to promise a client a date and a length. Thinks in deliverables
and sign-off: who approved this, when, against what. Has used Frame.io,
Notion boards, and a lot of email. Wants a tool that shows her the state of a
piece without asking the artist. Polite, exact, time-boxed.

**Job to be done.** Get a cinematic spine composed and its structure checked
so she can put a beat list in front of the client for approval this week —
and see, on the shelf, where the project stands.

**What good looks like.** The cinematic template says it is a stage, not a
length. The studio header states discipline · template · runtime. The shelf
row shows research/script state truthfully after she composes.

**Pet peeves.** A "locked" or "done" badge nobody earned. A step that reports
nothing to the shelf so she has to open it to know. Losing the composed spine
when the artist reopens a pick.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: chasing the artist for a beat list, formatting it, one review
round — **~1.5 days of calendar time**. She will adopt a tool where a composed
spine is on screen and stated on the shelf within **one 30-minute sitting**.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
A senior producer refuses a status she cannot trace to an act. Every state the
shelf shows for research and script must correspond to something that happened
(a spine composed, a candidate adopted), and reopening must be visible.

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. After create, the studio header names discipline, template and runtime.
2. After composing the spine, the shelf's research cell changes state, or the
   product says it does not report.
3. Reopening a composed spine is visible on the Script step (stale/frozen
   statement), not silent.
4. The structure report lists findings she can hand to an artist.
5. Reload lands on the step she was on.

**Surface binding.** `/projects` shelf, `/projects/new` (trailer → cinematic),
Research BeatVariantBoard, Script TrailerScript + StructurePanel. Never Frames.

**References.** Producer review-loop practice (Frame.io / Notion shot lists);
cinematic band from the repo's own beat sheet. Training-data grounded, marked.
