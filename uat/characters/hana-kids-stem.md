# Hana — the kids' STEM channel maker

**Segment:** two-person team, craft-first, maker + reviewer (her partner
checks every script for age fit). 4–6 minute STEM videos for 8–12 year olds;
~60k subscribers.

**Background / voice.** Former primary-school science coordinator. Every
video has one question a ten-year-old actually asked. She is ruthless about
reading level and about not lying to children with a simplification that is
false. Uses Canva, a shared doc, and a whiteboard. Cheerful, exact, protective.

**Job to be done.** Create a mid-length educational project, keep only the
facts a ten-year-old can hold, cut the speculative takes entirely, and adopt
the candidate whose beats she can say aloud to a child.

**What good looks like.** Conclusions and the "hottest take" are opt-in and
clearly marked as speculation so she can leave them out. The beats are
readable as sentences. The runtime she set (240 s) is respected or the
mismatch is stated.

**Pet peeves.** Speculation dressed as fact. A "hottest take" that sneaks into
scope. Beats written for adults presented as the only candidates.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: research, simplify, draft, partner review — **~4 hours per
video**. She will adopt a tool that gets her a sourced, speculation-free beat
outline in **under 40 minutes**.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
A senior children's science educator refuses any claim that is false-when-
simplified and any speculation presented at the same weight as fact. The
scope must let her exclude every conclusion and the script must honour that
exclusion visibly.

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. Conclusions default to not-taken and the wizard says so.
2. Leaving the hottest take out is one gesture and is reflected in Coverage.
3. The steel-man's "always travels" is explained on the card.
4. Beat text is readable in the beats modal before adoption.
5. Her runtime override (240 s) survives to the studio header.

**Surface binding.** `/projects/new` (educational → mid-educational-video →
runtime override → preset), Research guided (takes, conclusions, review),
Script guided duel + beats modal + Coverage. Never Frames.

**References.** Children's STEM content practice (reading level, one question
per video). Training-data grounded, marked.
