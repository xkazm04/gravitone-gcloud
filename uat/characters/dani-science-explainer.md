# Dani — the science explainer

**Segment:** solo creator, technical. YouTube channel of 8–15 minute science
explainers ("why does a bridge sing in the wind"), ~120k subscribers, publishes
every two weeks.

**Background / voice.** Physics PhD who left the lab because the papers had no
readers. Writes every script alone in a Google Doc, 3–5 sources per claim,
records the same evening. Has tried two AI script tools and stopped when both
produced a "hook → three facts → outro" skeleton that could have been about
anything. Talks in mechanisms, not takes. Dislikes being told a thing is
"engaging".

**Job to be done.** Turn a topic into a mid-length explainer script whose
argument has a real turn in it — a thing the viewer believed at 0:00 that is
false by 8:00 — with the facts and mechanisms that carry it laid out so he can
pick which ones travel.

**What good looks like.** He can see the notebook's mechanisms as candidates,
keep the ones that carry the turn, drop the ones that are decoration, and get a
script whose beats reference what he kept. The research must be about HIS topic,
or say plainly that it is not.

**Pet peeves.** Generic structure dressed as insight. A "research" step that
returns something he did not ask for. Facts with no source he can open.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: research (~3 h) plus outlining and drafting a 10-minute script
(~4 h) is **~7 hours per video**. He will adopt a tool that gets him to a
reviewable beat outline of his own topic in **under 40 minutes**, and will not
adopt one that saves 6 hours on a topic that is not his.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
A senior science communicator would refuse a script whose central mechanism is
wrong or unsourced, and would refuse structure that is not earned by the
material. The candidate scripts must argue from the notebook, name their turn,
and their claims must trace to the evidence log.

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. Creating an educational, mid-length project takes under 2 minutes and needs
   nothing he does not already know.
2. The research step tells him, before he decides anything, whether the
   notebook is about his topic.
3. Mechanism-level material is offered as choices he can keep or cut, and the
   consequence of a cut is shown.
4. A candidate script names its turn and its beats are readable before he
   adopts it.
5. His adopted candidate survives a reload.

**Surface binding.** `/projects/new` (educational → mid-educational-video →
preset → name), Research (guided or expert, educational), Script (Candidates,
Coverage). Never opens Frames.

**References.** Script-writing time anchors from creator forums and StudioBinder
(1–10 h per script by length/complexity); educational delivery 130–150 wpm.
Training-data grounded, marked.
