# Lena — the personal-finance Shorts creator

**Segment:** solo creator, semi-technical. Cuts 30–45 second Shorts/Reels
derived from her own longer videos; ~200k followers across platforms; posts
daily.

**Background / voice.** Ex-bank product manager. Her whole format is "one
number that changes how you see your money". She measures everything: retention
at 3 s, at 15 s, replays. She has a spreadsheet of hooks. She wants tools that
give her a derived short FROM a longer argument, not a new script from zero.
Brisk, numerical, slightly suspicious of anything that claims to be creative.

**Job to be done.** From a longer explainer's research, get a ≤45 s derived
short with one claim, one hook, one payoff — and know it fits the length
before she records.

**What good looks like.** The short-form template respects the seconds she
typed. The candidate for a derived short is visibly derived — one claim, not a
compressed essay. Word count vs seconds is shown.

**Pet peeves.** A 30-second target that yields a 90-second script. Hooks that
are questions nobody asked. Having to re-research what she already researched
for the long version.

**Motivation (time-saved) — quoted verbatim every run.**
The manual way: deriving a short from a long script — picking the claim,
re-hooking, trimming to 45 s, timing it aloud — is **~50 minutes per short**,
and she makes five a week. She will adopt a tool that gets her a timed,
readable short candidate in **under 10 minutes**.

**Senior-quality bar — quoted verbatim every run.**
A senior short-form editor refuses a short that opens with context instead of
the claim, and refuses one that runs over. The derived candidate must open on
the claim, fit the runtime at a stated wpm, and not invent a number the long
version's research does not hold.

**Scored acceptance criteria** (identical every run):
1. Creating a short-form-clip project seeds the runtime from the template and
   keeps the number she overrides.
2. The Script step shows a candidate that is a DERIVED short, and says what it
   was derived from.
3. Words vs seconds (at a stated wpm) is visible on the candidate.
4. A candidate that overruns her runtime says so in seconds.
5. Her runtime choice is visible on the studio header after create.

**Surface binding.** `/projects/new` (educational → short-form-clip → preset),
Research (guided), Script Candidates (derived-short), Spend bar. Never Frames.

**References.** Shorts arc = hook/build/payoff in 15–60 s; short-form
retention practice. Training-data grounded, marked.
