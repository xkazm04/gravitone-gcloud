# From nothing to a composed script

## Goal (user POV)
"I have an idea for a video and an afternoon. I want to name the thing, tell the
studio what kind of video it is, get the material in front of me as choices I
can make quickly, and walk out with a script whose beats and topics I chose —
without writing it from a blank page. I am not rendering a single image today."

## Definition of done (user POV)
1. I created the project and the studio opened on it, under the name I typed,
   as the kind of video I said it was.
2. The type question was answerable in my vocabulary — I did not have to know
   the studio's words for what I make.
3. Research gave me the material as decisions (cards, picks), not as a wall of
   text, and every decision I made was kept.
4. I could tell what the research was ABOUT — mine, or a stand-in — before I
   made decisions against it.
5. Script showed me candidate shapes for MY video, with beats/topics I can
   read, and I could adopt one (or compose one) and know it was adopted.
6. If I come back tomorrow the project is where I left it — type, research
   decisions, adopted script.
7. Nothing forced me into frames, rendering, or spending money.

## Scenarios in scope
- S1 educational, mid-length (facts → notebook → takes/conclusions → candidates)
- S2 educational, short-form clip (the derived-short candidate)
- S3 trailer (beat picks → compose spine → editable cut)
- S4 free discipline that must choose facts or beats (ModeChooser)
- S5 first-time account with NO locked style — the style stage's honest state
- S6 return visit: reload the studio and find the work
- S7 guided ⇄ expert face switch discards nothing

## Out of scope (this run)
Frames, Score, Cut. Any image or music generation. Recalibration is optional
(a real model turn, minutes) — exercised only where the Character's job needs a
rebalance, never as a gate on "done".
