# Declined proposals

Each entry: the proposal, the evidence behind it, and the reason it was refused. **A declined idea
cannot return as a fresh one without new evidence** — and where a reopening condition exists, it is
named, so the next person knows exactly what would have to be true.

## Run 1 · 2026-08-12 · six declines, all on the lens layer

Ruled by Fable in `runs/2026-08-12-l1-first-sweep/LENSES.md`. Adopted by the orchestrator without
amendment. Full per-area reasoning and ceilings live in that document; this is the index.

### 1 · A geopolitics lens — DECLINED

**Refused request:** the `politics` column absorbing the board, `the-number` without a referent, an
OBSERVED evidence floor.
**Why:** the predicted collapse did not occur in any of the six geopolitics seats. Four filed 7/7,
one 5/7, and the single low score (3/7) was ruled an **age** effect, not an area effect. No seat
reported `politics ≥ 50%` of cards — the dial the hypothesis itself nominated. Every geopolitics
blocker is a schema field or a prompt paragraph, each filed `content` by its own author.
**Reopens if:** a geopolitics topic produces a column concentration the derived-domain mechanism
(E3) cannot express.

### 2 · A tech lens — DECLINED

**Refused request:** practitioner consensus demoted below a vendor's MEASURED.
**Why:** the premise is dead. The ladder does not govern notebooks (RETRACTION 1), and the live axis
(`confidence`) has an *anti-vendor* default — the opposite of the fear. `software-eng`, seated
specifically to make this claim, refuted it twice and reported refuting it: *"If a lens shows up for
my area in the final ruling, it did not come from me."*
**Reopens if:** `DIRECTOR-DIMENSION.md`'s queued four-rung ladder ships into the research path, which
*would* create the demotion. Cheapest fixed in the proposal, before it lands.

### 3 · A fraud lens — DECLINED (both claims CONFIRMED)

**Refused request:** split `counter-case` from right-of-reply; cap the leap and type the falsifier
for named living people.
**Why — the most instructive result in the run:** both claims are *correct* and neither justifies a
lens. The counter-case split is the run's only `lens` filing and **exceeds what a lens can fix by
its own author's ceiling** — the lens template has no slot for modelling two parties and a record.
And the naming hazard was found by 12+ findings across **all four areas**, which is precisely why it
fails a per-domain bar. `public-corruption`, the most exposed seat: *"a fork would leave the other
nineteen seats shipping motive claims about named people with no handle on it."*
**Both are adopted as shared mechanism** (VERDICT E6, E11/E4).
**Reopens if:** an L2 run produces a concrete case where the subject-keyed shared rule is
demonstrably miscalibrated *for one area*. A per-area policy **constant** is admissible on that
evidence and on nothing weaker.

### 4 · An entertainment lens — DECLINED

**Refused request:** a `READ` rung for interpretive evidence — verifiable by examining the work.
**Why:** refuted by the seat purpose-built to prove it. `creator-economy` found OBSERVED
(*"read off a specific moment… `source · [mm:ss]` + the quoted line"*) fits its method exactly, that
its 40-video table is MEASURED by the repo's own definition, that the whole craft library is built
by its method — and that **no downstream rule privileges MEASURED**. It hunted for the demotion rule
and reported finding none. The proposed rung would duplicate an existing one.
**Reopens if:** a rule privileging MEASURED is introduced downstream.

### 5 · An evidence-age lens — DECLINED before it was requested

**Refused request:** none — declined pre-emptively, because `news-reaction` proved age is the real
predictive axis and the obvious next move would be to build a lens on it.
**Why:** age fails the lens bar for the same reason area does, in the opposite direction — it is a
property of the **topic-at-a-moment**, not of a beat, and it cuts *within* every beat. The correct
form is a per-run declaration (`fresh | developing | settled | accruing`) gating existing mandates:
one field and a handful of conditional rules, not a pack of columns.
**Ceiling accepted:** the maturity state does not remove fabrication pressure at 11:40. Only E1's
null path does.

### 6 · An exposure lens — DECLINED before it was requested

**Why:** same shape. Exposure is a per-run declaration on `Conclusion` (the `subject` field and its
keyed rules, E6), where it protects the OSINT seat and the music seat on the same day it protects
fraud.
**Ceiling accepted:** does not price **collateral** subjects — a named source inside an unnamed
story. No artifact in run 1 tested that. L2 work.
