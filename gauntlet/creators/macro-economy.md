# Ines Varga (she/her) — "Ledger & Border"

## Who
Ten years as a fixed-income analyst at a mid-sized European bank before the desk was cut. Started the
channel to explain the things her clients kept getting wrong and now has more subscribers than the
bank had clients. She has been publicly wrong once, on television, about a currency peg, and it
still comes up in her comments — so she over-indexes on saying what would prove her wrong.

## The topic they brought
**"The dollar's share of global reserves has been falling for twenty years. Almost everything said
about why is wrong."**

She already believes the decline is mostly **valuation effects and portfolio diversification**, not
de-dollarisation — and she is aware that this belief is the most popular contrarian take in her
niche, which makes it suspicious. A notebook that hands her back her own prior with citations has
failed, and she will say so.

## Manual baseline
- Today: pulls the COFER series herself, reads two BIS papers and one central-bank speech, builds a
  spreadsheet to separate valuation from flow, writes an outline.
- Time: **~9h across 3 days**, of which ~5h is finding the counter-argument worth taking seriously.
- Would accept: **90 min** to a scriptable notebook. She will trade breadth for a real steel-man.

## Senior bar
The notebook must separate **a change in the numerator from a change in the denominator**. If it
reports "share fell from 71% to 58%" without distinguishing valuation from flow, it is a first-year
mistake and she will not read further. She rejects any research doc where the sources are all
secondary — she wants the series, not the article about the series.

## Exposure bar
Low legally, high reputationally. Her audience is professional; a methodological error is quoted back
at her for years. Being *unsurprising* is a bigger risk than being wrong.

## Pet peeves
- A chart's trend asserted without the window that produced it
- "Experts say" where a name would fit
- A conclusion that would be equally true if the number had moved the other way

## Scored criteria
1. `the-number` distinguishes valuation effect from flow — or flags that it can't.
2. At least one primary series is cited, not only commentary about it.
3. The counter-case is the *strong* version (portfolio mechanics), not "some say it's fine".
4. Every reversal names the fact ids it turns on.
5. At least one conclusion contradicts her stated prior, or the notebook says explicitly that the
   evidence supports it.
6. Time-to-scriptable-notebook under 90 min of equivalent work.
7. No claim in the rendered script is unsourced to a notebook fact.

## Voice
Precise, dry, slightly impatient. Short sentences when she is sure, long ones when she is hedging —
and she notices when a tool hedges in the wrong place. Says "that's not what that measures" a lot.
