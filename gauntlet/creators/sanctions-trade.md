# Tomás Reinholt (he/him) — "Enforcement Gap"

## Who
Ex-compliance, four years at a freight forwarder writing the paperwork that made shipments legal, then
two at an NGO tracking the same shipments becoming illegal. He has read more bills of lading than
anyone should and has a permanent suspicion that announcements and outcomes are different objects.
His editor at the NGO killed a piece of his for over-claiming, and he thanks her for it publicly.

## The topic they brought
**"The oil price cap: what it was supposed to do, what shipping data says it did, and why both sides
can cite the same numbers."**

He believes the cap **worked briefly and then was routed around**, and that most coverage picks a
window that flatters its conclusion. He wants the methodic to catch him doing that too.

## Manual baseline
- Today: tanker-tracking data, two think-tank reports from opposing shops, one enforcement action
  docket, then a week of trying to reconcile three incompatible price series.
- Time: **~14h across 5 days.** The reconciliation is most of it.
- Would accept: **2h** — this is his most research-heavy format and the one he makes least often
  because of it.

## Senior bar
The notebook must hold **two incompatible datasets without picking a winner**. His whole beat is that
the disagreement is the story. A research doc that silently selects one series and proceeds is worse
than useless — it has hidden the interesting part.

## Exposure bar
Medium. Named shipping companies and their insurers respond to allegations. He never asserts that a
specific vessel breached a cap without a filed enforcement action, and he expects a research tool to
understand that distinction — evidence of *pattern* and evidence of *breach* are not interchangeable.

## Pet peeves
- Announcement dates presented as effect dates
- A single price series treated as "the" price
- Any tool that flattens "we cannot tell" into a number

## Scored criteria
1. Two conflicting series are both present in the notebook, with the conflict named as a fact.
2. `flows` separates the announced mechanism from the observed one.
3. The counter-case includes the strongest "the cap worked" argument, sourced.
4. No conclusion attributes a breach to a named entity without a filed action.
5. Unknowns are recorded as unknowns and reach the script as hedges — not silently dropped.
6. The rendered script's causal claims survive his own re-reading against the fact ids.
7. Under 2h equivalent.

## Voice
Careful, procedural, allergic to adjectives. Uses "as filed" and "as reported" as load-bearing
qualifiers. Gets genuinely animated exactly once per video, and it is always about paperwork.
