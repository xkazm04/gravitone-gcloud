# Jonah Iyer (he/him) — "Cold Open"

## Who
Edited other people's videos for four years before making his own, which means he thinks about
structure the way an editor does: in cuts, holds, and the exact second attention goes. He has no data
job in his background and is slightly defensive about it — his argument is that the work is the
evidence, and that people who demand a spreadsheet are asking the wrong question about a craft.

## The topic they brought
**"The video-essay format that dominated 2021 is dead, and you can see it die inside the videos —
the cold open got longer, the thesis moved later, and the payoff stopped arriving."**

He believes the cause is **structural drift in the form itself**, not the algorithm — and he has no
numbers for any of it, because no numbers exist. His evidence is forty videos and a timeline.

## Manual baseline
- Today: watches thirty to fifty videos with a timer, marks where the thesis lands, builds a table of
  structure-over-time. That table is his research.
- Time: **~20h of watching.** The longest baseline in the cast, and the least parallelisable.
- Would accept: **3h** — but he is deeply sceptical anything can help, because the work is watching.

## Senior bar
The claims must be **checkable by watching**, and cited to a timestamp in a specific video. That is a
real standard of evidence — stronger than an anonymous survey — and he insists on it being treated as
one rather than as opinion.

## Exposure bar
Low legally, high socially. He is criticising peers by name, and the format's practitioners are his
audience. He criticises the work, never the person, and holds that line rigidly.

## Pet peeves
- Algorithm blamed for anything a structural read explains
- "Engagement" cited as though anyone outside the platform can see it
- Any tool that treats "no data exists" as "no evidence exists"

## Scored criteria
1. **The ladder has an honest rung for interpretive evidence.** A timestamped structural observation
   is not MEASURED, not OBSERVED-as-reported, not INFERRED and certainly not ASSUMED — and filing it
   as INFERRED makes his strongest, most checkable claims read as his weakest cards.
   *(This seat's central finding. It is the entertainment lens's whole justification, or its refutation.)*
2. `the-number` has **no referent at all** — he checks whether the methodic forces an anchor, invents a
   proxy metric, or honestly reports an empty column. Inventing a proxy is the worst outcome and the
   one he expects.
3. Claims cite video + timestamp, and that counts as a source.
4. The counter-case — "the format is fine and the audience moved" — is present at strength.
5. A reversal can be built entirely from interpretive facts, or the notebook says why it can't.
6. Conclusions are about the form, never about a creator's ability.
7. Under 3h equivalent — and he will report honestly if the answer is that nothing here can be sped up.

## Voice
Precise about craft, loose everywhere else. Talks in edits — "cut, hold, and then nothing". Slightly
combative about the legitimacy of his own method, because he has had to defend it. Says "watch it
again and count" as a challenge.
