# Ivo Petrič (he/him) — "Removed From Platform"

## Who
Media analyst at a research shop for five years, laid off in a consolidation he had written a note
predicting. He is the rare person who finds licensing accounting genuinely interesting and has built
an audience of people who have started to find it interesting too. He is careful with forecasts
because his old job made him publish them and he watched what happened.

## The topic they brought
**"Shows are being deleted from the platforms that made them, and the explanation isn't cost-cutting —
it is a tax and amortisation treatment that makes an unwatched show worth more gone."**

He believes the mechanism is **specific, documented in filings, and almost never named correctly** in
coverage, which reaches for "greed" because the accounting is boring.

## Manual baseline
- Today: filings with content-amortisation disclosures, two analyst notes, and a list of removals he
  maintains himself because nobody publishes a good one.
- Time: **~9h**, and building the removals list is ongoing rather than per-video.
- Would accept: **90 min**, assuming the removals data is supplied.

## Senior bar
The notebook must get the **accounting mechanism right, in the filings' own terms**. His entire
differentiation is that he names the line item. A research doc that says "for tax reasons" without
saying which treatment is exactly the coverage he exists to replace.

## Exposure bar
Low legally; high with a narrow expert audience who will correct an accounting error immediately and
publicly. He would rather be slow than corrected on a mechanism.

## Pet peeves
- "For tax reasons" with no specifics
- Subscriber numbers used as a proxy for anything financial
- Write-downs described as losses of cash

## Scored criteria
1. The accounting mechanism is named at line-item level, sourced to a filing.
2. Write-down, impairment and cash cost are distinct facts.
3. `macro` has a real referent here (rates, capital cost) — he is a test of whether the market lens's
   macro column generalises to a non-market topic or was Bitcoin-specific.
4. The counter-case — "this is ordinary catalogue management" — is present with its source.
5. No conclusion attributes a removal decision to a named executive.
6. Falsifiers are future filings.
7. Under 90 min equivalent.

## Voice
Dry, precise, faintly weary about being the person who has to explain amortisation again. Warms up
when a filing footnote confirms something. Says "which is the whole trick" when the mechanism lands.
