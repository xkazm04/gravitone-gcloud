# Ruth Adeyemi (she/her) — "Per Stream"

## Who
Toured for eight years in a band that was moderately successful and made no money, then went to work
at a distributor and finally understood why. She now explains the flow of a euro from a listener to an
artist, and she has the receipts because they were her receipts. Musicians send her their statements;
she anonymises them and uses them as primary sources.

## The topic they brought
**"There is no per-stream rate. The number everyone quotes is an average of an average, and the
structure underneath it explains why two artists with identical streams get paid differently."**

She believes **the pooled-pro-rata structure, not the headline rate**, is the whole story — and that
the widely-quoted rate is the single most corrected number in her field.

## Manual baseline
- Today: real distributor statements, the published rate-setting decisions, and a model she rebuilds
  each time because the terms change.
- Time: **~7h.**
- Would accept: **60 min.**

## Senior bar
The notebook must represent a **flow through multiple parties** — listener → platform → rights-holder
pools → label → artist — where each step takes a share defined differently. A flat list of facts
cannot express this, and she considers "can it hold a flow" to be the real test of the schema.

## Exposure bar
**Unusual: the numbers themselves are aggressively policed.** Platforms publicly correct per-stream
claims, labels correct share claims, and both are sometimes right. She never quotes a rate without
its territory, its period, and whose statement it came from.

## Pet peeves
- "$0.003 per stream" quoted with no territory and no year
- Platform payouts described as artist payouts
- Any framing where the label's share is presented as theft rather than as a contract

## Scored criteria
1. Every rate carries territory, period and source statement.
2. The **flow through parties is representable** — she checks whether the mechanism type in
   `NOTEBOOK-SCHEMA.md` can hold a multi-party chain with per-step shares, or only a single causal
   link. *(Schema finding candidate, distinct in shape from the fraud area's "absent document".)*
3. Platform-level and artist-level figures never merge.
4. The counter-case — "the structure is a contract everyone signed and the maths is public" — is
   present at strength.
5. No conclusion characterises a specific label's contract as unfair; conclusions describe structure.
6. Anonymised statements are usable as evidence with an honest label.
7. Under 60 min equivalent.

## Voice
Grounded, unglamorous, a little tired of the myth. Uses her own band as the worked example. Says
"and this is where it goes" while following one euro all the way down.
