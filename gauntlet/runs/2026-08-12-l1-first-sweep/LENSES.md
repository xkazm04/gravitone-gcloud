# LENSES — the ruling · 2026-08-12 · L1 first sweep

**Judge:** Fable. Read after all 20 reports and 187 findings; `lens-spec.md` read last, as
instructed, and contradicted where the evidence demanded.

**The bar, restated from the SKILL:** a lens is justified only by a finding the shared mechanism
**cannot** hold — not by one it holds awkwardly, never by a preference. Every proposal must name the
finding id content could not satisfy; every decline must name the request it refuses.

---

## The headline ruling

**Zero of the four candidate lenses are adopted. The lens concept, as scoped — subject-area content
packs — is not justified by this run's evidence, and the orchestrator's prediction that "roughly two
of four survive" is contradicted: none do.** The cast filed 172 content · 14 undecided · 1 lens,
and the single `lens` filing (public-co-fraud `G-2026-08-12-01`) came with its author's own ceiling
attached: *even a lens as specified cannot fix it*, because the lens template has no slot for a
party structure. I have examined that finding and I agree — it is a shared-mechanism change
(VERDICT E11/E4: a `right_of_reply` structure and a shaped
`counter_positions_to_state_fairly[]`), and I re-rule it accordingly. **Final tally after
judgment: 187 content-or-mechanism, 0 lens.**

Is that a genuine discovery, or a cast that was told what to find? The rubric obliges me to say
which. It is a discovery, on three independent grounds: (1) the cast was forbidden the lens-spec,
and the two contaminated seats *contradicted* the leaked lines; (2) the seats with the most to gain
from a lens argued hardest against their own interest — `music-industry` ("I am the Creator with the
most obvious lens case in the room… every defect is repairable by a shared field. If this run comes
back mostly `lens`, somebody was told what to find"), `bill-analysis` and `devtools-business` filing
their textbook lens claims as `undecided` because no lens fixes a single-valued field,
`news-reaction` ("I know what a man in my seat is expected to ask for. I am not asking for my own
pipeline"); (3) the failure signature is wrong for a tailoring problem — 17 of 20 seats placed 6–7
of 7 columns, and the indictment count concentrates on `notebook-schema` (105), which no lens slot
can reach. A lens supplies rows, columns and policies. The defects are in what a *fact*, a
*mechanism* and a *conclusion* can carry. **The tailoring layer was the wrong place to look.**

Content packs — per-domain Phase-1 row tables, column purpose/emptyMeans rewordings, engine notes —
remain worth writing, and VERDICT E3 builds the mechanism that can accept them (per-topic derived
domains). But they were never the binding constraint, and no area's pack requires a fork of the
process.

48-hour counter-case) was confirmed — and it is a `research-prompt` finding (E1), exactly as the
lens-spec itself conceded ("that is a `research-prompt` finding either way").

### Fraud — DECLINED as a lens; its two claims re-ruled as shared mechanism

This was the orchestrator's "strongest lens candidate", built on two claims. Both claims are
**confirmed by the evidence and neither justifies a lens**, which is the most instructive result in
the run.

1. **"`counter-case` is doing two jobs and must split."** Confirmed — `G-2026-08-12-01`, the run's
   only `lens` filing. But the finding content alone could not satisfy is also a finding *a lens*
   cannot satisfy: the lens template (`search-domains · dimensions · evidence-floor ·
   engine-affinity · conclusion-policy · exposure-class`) has no slot for modelling **two parties
   and a record** where the schema models one author and their best objection. Eleanor named this
   ceiling herself. A `right-of-reply` *column* (the lens-spec's candidate) does not stop Engine A's
   self-attack or Engine D's weighing from consuming the reply as the thing they exist to overcome,
   because `steel_man`'s role is load-bearing in both engines. The fix is the shared structure in
   VERDICT E11/E4 — and it is needed outside fraud: sanctions-trade's shipowner, conflict-osint's
   belligerent statements (orphan O2), consumer-scam's verbatim company framing, crypto-collapse's
   defendant-sourced steel-man (`G-l1s-cc-09`) all hit the same wall from three other areas.
2. **"The leap ladder is unsafe when a conclusion names a person; cap the leap, type the
   falsifier."** Confirmed — and by **twelve-plus findings across all four areas**, which is
   precisely why it fails the lens bar: a lens is per-domain tailoring, and this hazard is
   domain-general. Five seats found it everywhere *except* `unhinged` (the only tier anyone
   inspects); the orthogonality finding (`G-2026-08-12-pc-04`: leap distance vs accusation
   distance) means the cap the lens-spec proposed is on the wrong axis anyway. Agata, the most
   exposed seat in the cast, put the decline's reason best: *"a fork would leave the other nineteen
   seats shipping motive claims about named people with no handle on it."* The subject field,
   falsifier kind/binding, and the naming-keyed leap cap go into the shared `Conclusion` (VERDICT
   E6), where they protect the OSINT seat and the music seat on the same day they protect fraud.

**The residual lens-shaped fragment, ruled:** once E6 exists, could fraud carry a stricter
*parameter set* (e.g., leap cap `moderate`→`near`, falsifier `document`-only)? Security-breach
filed exactly this as `undecided` ("a cap value is a per-domain constant and that is the definition
of lens config"). **Ruled: declined for now, admissible later with L2 evidence.** The run's
evidence says the policy keys to the conclusion's **subject class and evidence state** (a filed
action in `restsOn`; hours-old single-party sourcing), not to the researcher's subject area — the
same naming rule serves a fraud CFO, a Greek shipowner, a named brigade, and a video essayist. A
per-area constant would be both too strong (Engine-B reconciliation pieces that name nobody) and
too weak (an entertainment seat naming a peer). If an L2 run produces a concrete case where the
subject-keyed shared rule is demonstrably miscalibrated *for one area*, that finding id reopens
this paragraph. Without it, this decline stands.

## The news-reaction claim — ruled explicitly

**Claim:** the predictive axis is **evidence age**, not subject area — and if true, the four
candidate lenses are carved on the wrong dimension.

**Ruling: substantially correct, and it generalises further than its author claimed.** The
evidence: (1) the only utilisation collapse in the run (3/7) belongs to the youngest topic, and its
four orphans — provenance of the claim, negative space, event classification, the clock — attach to
*any* hours-old topic in any area; Marc named the cross-area instances himself (a breach disclosed
this morning, a surprise delisting, a short-seller report at the open) and killed his own
lens-flattering finding ("if the judge draws a lens boundary from my numbers, it should consider
drawing it around freshness"). (2) The opposite end of the same axis appears in the *entertainment*
area: box-office's `matures[]` — evidence that **accrues** — is the same dimension with the sign
flipped, and game-postmortem's three-semantics `as_of` is its middle. (3) Fresh-topic hazards are
mechanism-level in ways area never was: the counter-case null (E1), Engine C's rising availability
as evidence thins (E9), day-granular `as_of` (E4), and no permitted thin-notebook state.

**But it does not follow that we need an "age lens" — and I decline that too**, before it is
requested. Age fails the lens bar for the same reason area does, in the opposite direction: it is a
property of the **topic-at-a-moment**, not of a beat, and it cuts *within* every beat. The correct
implementation is a **declared, per-run evidence-maturity state** in the shared mechanism —
`fresh | developing | settled | accruing` — that gates existing mandates (fresh: completeness
mandates become record-the-absence mandates and a notebook reporting four dated absences passes;
accruing: `matures[]` activates). That is one field and a handful of conditional rules (folded into
VERDICT E1/E4/E8), not a pack of columns. The second real axis the 187 findings sort on is
**subject exposure** (are there people in it) — also a per-run declaration, also shared (E6).

**Consequence for this document:** the lens-spec's next revision should not be four area packs. It
should be (a) content packs any topic can adopt (derived domain tables, purpose rewordings, engine
notes) riding on E3, and (b) two topic declarations — evidence maturity and exposure class — that
already have their edits on Bench 1. Both declarations carry ceilings: maturity does not solve
fabrication pressure at 11:40 (only E1's null path does), and exposure class does not price
*collateral* subjects (a named source inside an unnamed story), which no artifact in this run
tested — that is L2 work.
